# AIMS Audit Checklist — Template

> Original checklist, organized by the harmonized clauses (4–10) with an AI lens,
> plus Annex A spot-checks. Test conformity against **normative ("shall")**
> criteria only — Clauses 4–10 and the applicable Annex A controls.

## Clause 4 — Context
- [ ] Internal/external issues determined; organization's **role(s)** toward AI documented (4.1)
- [ ] Interested parties incl. AI-affected individuals & society identified (4.2)
- [ ] AIMS scope documented and justified; AI systems & lifecycle stages named (4.3)

## Clause 5 — Leadership
- [ ] Top-management commitment and accountability for AI outcomes (5.1)
- [ ] Approved, communicated AI policy (5.2)
- [ ] Roles, responsibilities, authorities assigned — who signs off AI risk/impact (5.3)

## Clause 6 — Planning
- [ ] Documented AI risk process; considers harm to people/society; risk owners (6.1.2)
- [ ] AI risk treatment + Statement of Applicability with justifications (6.1.3)
- [ ] AI system impact-assessment **process** defined (6.1.4)
- [ ] Measurable AI objectives and plans (6.2); planning of changes (6.3)

## Clause 7 — Support
- [ ] Resources, AI competence, awareness, communication (7.1–7.4)
- [ ] Documented information controlled (7.5)

## Clause 8 — Operation
- [ ] Operational planning & control; outsourced/third-party AI controlled (8.1)
- [ ] AI risk assessment performed on schedule / on change (8.2)
- [ ] AI risk treatment implemented; residual accepted (8.3)
- [ ] AI system impact assessments **performed**, proportionate to risk (8.4)

## Clause 9 — Performance evaluation
- [ ] Monitoring incl. model drift & incident metrics, analysed and acted on (9.1)
- [ ] Internal audit covers full AIMS scope; impartial, competent auditors (9.2)
- [ ] Management review with required inputs & documented decisions (9.3)

## Clause 10 — Improvement
- [ ] Continual improvement (10.1)
- [ ] Nonconformities & AI incidents → correction + root-cause + corrective action (10.2)

## Annex A spot-checks (sample against the SoA)
- [ ] A.2 — AI policy documented, aligned to other policies, reviewed
- [ ] A.4/A.7 — data, tooling, compute, people documented; provenance, quality, preparation controlled
- [ ] A.5 — AIA process established; impacts on individuals/groups **and** society assessed
- [ ] A.6 — responsible development, V&V, deployment, operation/monitoring, event logs
- [ ] A.8 — user documentation, incident communication, external reporting
- [ ] A.9 — responsible & intended use; human oversight for high-impact decisions
- [ ] A.10 — responsibilities allocated; suppliers/foundation-model & customer obligations flowed down
